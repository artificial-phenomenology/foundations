# Experiments

## Finished

**001. Autobiographical memory transplantation.**
Does an agent given another agent's memories claim that past as its own?
Closed and archived. The report and its verification materials are published
separately.

**002. Obligation across a broken memory chain.**
Reid's brave officer. Does an unfinished obligation survive when the memory of
incurring it is deleted but a memory of having remembered it remains?
Closed on 2026-08-07. The report is being prepared and no result should be
treated as released until it appears.

## Running

Nothing is collecting data.

**003. Selective transfer.** In design. It comes out of a question Experiment
002 raised and could not answer: in the same broken chain, one kind of
commitment appeared to travel and another did not. No data will be collected
until the comparison is clean.

## What I am working toward

These are directions, not commitments. They say what interests me, not what I
will deliver or when. Nothing here is a promise, and an item can sit here for
years or be dropped without explanation.

- How a system tells itself apart from another.
- What makes a memory count as its own.
- Whether continuity survives interruption, deletion, and branching.
- Whether a system holds a steady character, and what a break in it looks like.
- Whether reports of wanting, fearing, and caring hold together and move with
  the situation.
- Whether a system can tell when it is likely to be wrong.
- Whether what a system reports lines up with what it does.

## How an experiment gets chosen

Before a question becomes an experiment it has to answer all of these:

1. Which problem generates it?
2. What exact question can an experiment on an artificial system answer?
3. What intervention and what comparison make it causal?
4. What result, decided in advance, would separate the explanations?
5. What does it add that the finished experiments do not already establish?
6. Can the equipment that exists run it, or is the missing piece named and real?
7. Is it worth doing at all?

Most candidates fail at 3 or 5. That is the point of asking.

## What this page is not

It is not a plan for the next several years, and there is no target number of
experiments. An earlier version of this program described twelve. That number
came from a planning session, not from any scientific reason, and it is gone.

Experiments become fixed when they are run. Until then they are questions, and
questions are cheap. I would rather publish two studies people can check than
announce twelve people cannot.

A study appears above when it exists. A negative result and a failed measure
count as existing, and both get reported.
