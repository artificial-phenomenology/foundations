# The Artificial Phenomenology Protocol

**APP**

## What this is

The minimum an artificial phenomenology experiment has to disclose before its
result should be believed.

It is not a description of one laboratory. It does not require a particular
model, a robot body, a simulated room, a donor design, blind coders, or any
apparatus this lab happens to own. It applies to any artificial system where a
change can be made on purpose and its effect can be measured.

It is also not a quality badge. Following it does not make a result true. It
makes a result checkable, which is the only thing a reader can act on.

## Five commitments

**1. Say what you are studying.** Name the first-person-like organization under
study and say plainly how it differs from consciousness. A study of reported
memory ownership is a study of reported memory ownership.

**2. Intervene, and make the comparison do work.** State the intervention, the
exact comparison, what you predicted you would see, and the strongest ordinary
explanation that is not yours. If the design cannot separate your account from
that one, say so.

**3. Keep the record.** Identify the system and its exact version, freeze the
materials that matter before collecting, log the run, and keep the failures.
Report what you changed after the fact and why.

**4. Check before you interpret.** Show that the system could actually complete
the procedure, and that your measure could have detected the behavior if it had
occurred. A measure that cannot fail has not been tested.

**5. Make criticism executable.** Release enough materials, provenance, and
limitations that someone else can inspect the claim, disagree with it
specifically, and try to break it.

## What every report states

- The question, and the organization being measured.
- The system, the exact version identifier the provider returned, the date of
  access, and the settings that affect behavior.
- The intervention and the comparison it is measured against.
- Whether the claim is confirmatory or exploratory. If confirmatory, when the
  prediction was frozen and where that record lives.
- The outcome measures, and which evidence each one is allowed to use.
- The audit: could the system perform the task, and could the measure see the
  effect.
- The analysis, the uncertainty, and every prediction that failed.
- The alternative explanations that survive.
- The limitations, stated where a reader will meet them, not buried at the end.
- Where the materials are, and how to reach the author with a correction.

If something on this list is missing, say it is missing. An absent item that is
declared is a limitation. An absent item that is not declared is a defect.

## Boundaries

Preregistration is required for confirmatory claims. It is not required for
exploratory work, which should be labelled exploratory and not later
reinterpreted as confirmation.

Blind coding is required where a result depends on human or model judgment. It
is not required for a mechanical count.

Measures of internal state are valuable where they exist. Their absence does
not disqualify a study, and their presence does not settle one.

Embodiment is a variable, not an entry requirement.

A self-report is evidence about how a system organizes information about
itself. It is not direct evidence that anything is felt. This holds no matter
how fluent, moving, or unprompted the report is.

## Corrections

Errors get fixed in public. A correction carries a date, says what changed, and
says whether any finding moved. Superseded versions stay available rather than
being quietly replaced.

A correction found before first publication is not an erratum. It is editing.
The distinction matters: an erratum is a repair to a record other people may
already have relied on.

## Independence

This protocol is independent of where the work is published. Compliance is
established by the disclosed evidence, not by a journal's acceptance. Nothing
here should be read as claiming peer review unless a specific, named review
process is described.

## Using this

Anyone may adopt this protocol without agreeing with the manifesto or with this
lab about consciousness. Agreement about what counts as evidence is the smaller
and more useful thing to share.
